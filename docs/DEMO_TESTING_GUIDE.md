# Test Share Yarana in five minutes

Open the public HTTPS demo link in Safari, Chrome or Firefox. Keep the whole test in the same browser on the same site address. Use fictional details and the built-in demo accounts. Share Yarana is free and connects people to plan trips together.

## Try a trip from both sides

1. **Choose a host.** Open **Switch Traveller** near the top of the page and select **Solo traveller · Meera Kulkarni**. Close the panel.
2. **Publish a trip.** Open **Create** and title it `Sunrise chai hike`. Enter `Sinhagad` as the destination and `Pune` as the starting city. Pick a future date for both start and end, select **Couples** under traveller groups and enter `4` for maximum participants. Leave available spots blank. Tap **Preview listing → Publish Trip**.
3. **Switch travellers.** Open **Switch Traveller** again and choose **Couple · Kavya & Dev**. Close the panel.
4. **Discover and request.** On Trip Details, use the **Back to trips** arrow to reach **Explore**. Search for `Sunrise chai hike`, open its card and choose **View trip details**. Tap **Request to Join → Send request**. Kavya & Dev request two spots. Open **View My Requests** and look for **Pending**.
5. **Accept as the host.** Switch the demo account back to **Meera Kulkarni**. Open **My Trips → Hosting**. Under `Sunrise chai hike`, choose **Manage requests**, then **Accept request → Confirm acceptance**. Check that **Spots left** shows `2`.
6. **Open the group.** Switch back to **Kavya & Dev**. In **My Trips → Join Requests**, look for **Accepted** and open **Trip Group Space**. In **Members**, check that you see Meera as host and Kavya & Dev as the accepted party. Try the checklist.
7. **Reset.** Open **Switch Traveller → Reset Demo Data → Confirm reset**. The published demo trip and your request changes are cleared from this browser. Saved drafts and bookmarks stay in place.

Trips and requests are shared across tabs on the same site address in the same browser profile. A different browser, device or domain has its own demo data. Private browsing is separate from your regular browser session. Switch accounts in the app to try both sides of the same trip. Use the main demo URL when inviting another tester.

## Send feedback

Include your device model, browser and the step you reached. For each question, give a rating from **1 (poor or difficult)** to **5 (excellent or easy)** and add a short note if something stood out.

1. **Navigation**. How easy was it to find Create, the demo switcher, Explore and My Trips?
2. **Visual design**. How easy was it to read text and spot the next action? What did you notice about the photos and layout?
3. **Trip creation**. How clear were the form, preview and publish steps? Was any field difficult to fill on your phone?
4. **Joining**. How easy was it to find the trip, request to join and understand the status?
5. **Trust and safety**. How clear was it that the people were fictional and results stayed in your browser?
6. **Overall usability**. How easy was it to finish the whole journey? Which one change would help most?

If you hit a problem, say what you tapped, what you expected and what happened. Use fictional information in any screenshot or feedback.

## Try photos, trip feedback and location pins

- In Community, use **See and share traveller photos**, then **Share a photo**. Choose a non-sensitive JPG, PNG or WebP, add a caption and confirm permission to share. **Post photo locally** adds it to the current browser's feed. Refresh, switch travellers, then switch back and remove your post.
- As an accepted traveller, open Trip Details and **Leave feedback**. Choose a rating and write feedback about the plan or your experience. Save, edit or remove your review. The host and pending travellers cannot review that trip.
- In Group Chat, choose **Share location**. Enter a meeting point or explicitly choose **Use current location**, then review it before **Add location to chat**. Test denying location permission and using a typed place instead. Pins persist locally until removed or reset. No live tracking runs; Google Maps opens only if you select its link.
- **Reset Demo Data** also removes photo posts, feedback and location pins. Drafts and bookmarks stay intact.

These are local previews, not public online posts or real messages. Different browsers and devices do not share them. Use non-sensitive sample photos and locations during testing.
